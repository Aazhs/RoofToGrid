The Methodology.

RoofToGrid utilizes a deterministic, four-phase approach to transform raw architectural space into utility-scale grid assets. Our process emphasizes precision engineering and rigorous data validation over heuristic estimates.

Phase 01

Analysis

Spatial and irradiance baselining. We extract geometric parameters and correlate them with localized meteorological datasets to establish theoretical yield limits.
